The Grounds and Exterior

One coherent site: the landscaped entrance in front, the tennis court to the left, the pool to the right of the residence and the guest cottage farther right.

Grand arrival

A generous driveway approaches the main residence, with lawns along both sides and a central landscaped strip. A circular fountain roundabout sits close to the entrance, with ample parking space.

Private tennis court

Positioned on the left side of the compound, surrounded by landscaping and privacy-conscious screening.

Resort-style swimming pool

An expansive luxury pool on the right side of the main residence, with a transparent glass-floor section looking down into the basement gym.

Independent guest cottage

A separate cottage farther to the right, away from the pool area, giving guests privacy and independent accommodation.

Garden and entertaining area

A backyard for intimate gatherings, with a barbecue setup and a summer-hut seating area.

Three Levels, Three Characters

Each floor has its own purpose. No two share a layout.

Level one

The Basement

A fully equipped private gym directly beneath the swimming pool. The transparent pool floor overhead lets natural light filter down into the space.

Level two

The Ground Floor

Floor-to-ceiling glass surrounds the main living areas.

  • An expansive living room
  • A large chef's kitchen
  • A very large dining area connected to the kitchen and living space
  • A cosy fireplace finished with marble stones
  • A private cinema room
  • A dedicated game center
  • A compact, sophisticated bar with a wine display
Level three

The Upper Floor

Six private bedrooms, each with its own toilet, bathroom and walk-in closet. A wrap-around terrace extends around the floor, supported by Corinthian pillars and beams that join classical detailing to the modern structure.

Above it all, a flat rooftop deck is set aside for peaceful relaxation and stargazing.

Water above, wellness below

An Extraordinary Perspective.

Where leisure and wellness exist in perfect harmony, a remarkable glass-floor pool creates a connection between the water above and the private fitness sanctuary below.
